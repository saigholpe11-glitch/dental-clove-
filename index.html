Meet The Founders & Clinicians

Three Dedicated Specialists, One Trusted Practice

Established in Sector 12, Noida in 2016, Dental Clove was founded with a clear mission: to bring transparent, ethical, and high-precision dental care to families across Noida and Delhi NCR.

Unlike large commercial dental chains where patients see a different visiting consultant each visit, Dental Clove is owned and directly run by our resident doctors. You always receive personalized attention from the same trusted specialists who know your oral health history.

Our Clinical Philosophy

We practice conservative dentistry: preserving your natural tooth structure whenever possible, explaining every diagnostic detail with intraoral photos, and providing clear, honest treatment estimates before beginning.
